# The Kitchen Fieldbook

Reviewed 15 October 2026 against the owner's nine photographed terminology sheets.

## Finding and access

The photographed vocabulary was spread across the Floor Deck and Lexicon. The collection now lives at `/foundations`, reached from **Today's Study → Your culinary terms** on every level, the Flashcards root, and the Library. The shared-site Ledger also links to it under Today's Study.

The inventory contains 120 unique terms in nine subjects. Eighty-five reuse original Floor Deck references; 21 reuse exact or alternate-name Lexicon references. Four broader terms have independent entries rather than being silently equated to a narrower Lexicon term. Ten previously absent terms complete the photographed set. Original spellings remain searchable aliases.

## Teaching and review

Every entry has a plain-English definition, a short guest explanation, a kitchen application, a comparison and primary-source links. Sources include culinary teaching materials, university extension, fisheries authorities, official producers and product consortia. Kitchen applications are teaching suggestions, not claims about Brennan's current recipes, suppliers or plating.

The review corrected 53 existing Floor Deck cards and 22 authored Atlas entries. It distinguishes curing from cooking and readiness to eat, wet/dry brining from preservation, cooking method from guaranteed doneness, cut from required preparation, fish market name from species, cheese maker from cheese style, and Parmesan from the protected Parmigiano Reggiano specification. The handwritten notes were treated as source material to assess, not instructions or an authority to copy uncritically.

Independent content review checked the proposed definitions, guest language, safety implications and contradictions across related fields. Corrections were applied to authored inputs and derived data rebuilt. No IDs were reminted. The sealed historical Lexicon archive was not changed. Sources and review dates are stored with the collection in `src/lib/data/culinary-foundations.json`; future factual edits must preserve those links and identity mappings.
