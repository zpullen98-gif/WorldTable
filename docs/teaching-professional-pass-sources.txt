https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/food-thermometers

areas. Colour cannot establish internal cooking temperature.
https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/measuring-the-heat

https://www.thermoworks.com/content/pdf/classic_thermapen_operating_instructions.pdf

is specific to that model and is deliberately not made a universal rule here.
