PREP TO PASS: LINKED LESSON EDITORIAL REVIEW
Reviewed 23 October 2026

Scope
Six directly connected Cuisine lessons: methods/boil-blanch-and-steam,
methods/frying, pastry/caramel, pastry/choux, butter-emulsions/beurre-blanc,
and brennans/shrimp-and-grits. Corrections include their recall cards,
multiple-choice answers and applicable ordering drills. Existing stems,
identities and grading histories remain intact. Folio joins were added by
the companion integration work. No sealed raw or archive files changed.

What changed
1. Blanching: test texture, distinguish cooling for preparation ahead from
   immediate finishing, and drain/dry before sauce. Removed claims that an ice
   bath permanently sets green colour. Applied the same distinction in the
   active lexiconDefinitions override, the blanching film watchFor text and
   the existing fd_0044 Floor Deck card (including its generated cloze).
2. Frying: golden crust is not the interior cooking endpoint. Manage batches
   and draining without a universal rack-only verdict. Commercial fryer fire
   instructions now refer to alarm, trained response and evacuation; a small
   pan lid instruction must not stand in for the commercial procedure.
3. Beurre blanc: reduction quantities depend on the formula and batch size.
   Temperature checks use a thermometer, not touching the pan. Removed an
   automatic four-hour warm-hold allowance; texture and verified holding
   controls are separate. The FDA figure is labelled as a model hot-holding
   minimum for applicable foods, not a universal emulsion temperature.
4. Choux: cooked-paste moisture controls egg demand; more egg does not promise
   a bigger puff. Teach consistency, steam expansion and sufficiently dried
   structure together, with faults treated as possibilities rather than a
   diagnosis made from a picture alone.
5. Sugar: separate syrup concentration from caramel browning; distinguish
   New Orleans creamy pecan pralines from French caramelised-nut preparations.
   Brennan's published Foster recipe starts with butter and brown sugar, so
   that sauce cannot act as a white-sugar colour standard.
6. Grit cake: ground maize and hominy grits are not universally synonymous.
   Preserve the saved menu's fried pimento cheese grit cake, but remove
   invented production ratios, thickness and chilling duration. Confirm the
   actual kitchen formula, coating and controlled cooling process. Shrimp
   C/O curl and elapsed minutes are not measured endpoints; the drill now
   asks for the verified process and appropriate thermometer.

Primary-source basis, checked 23 October 2026
Wegmans, Blanching and Shocking Vegetables:
https://www.wegmans.com/cooking-techniques/blanching-and-shocking-vegetables
Preparation ahead versus direct service; texture, cooling and draining.

US Fire Administration, Fire Extinguishers:
https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/fire-extinguishers/index.html
Training, escape route and Class K cooking-oil context. Kitchen emergency
procedures remain equipment- and site-specific.

Rouxbe, How to Make a Butter Sauce:
https://rouxbe.com/cooking-school/how-to-make-a-butter-sauce
Reduction, mounting and the need to manage a butter emulsion.

FDA Food Code 2022, sections 3-501.16 and 3-501.19:
https://www.fda.gov/media/164194/download?attachment
Hot holding and conditional time-as-control requirements. Local adoption and
the kitchen's verified procedure govern practice.

FDA, Selecting and Serving Fresh and Frozen Seafood Safely:
https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-fresh-and-frozen-seafood-safely
Cooking checks and supporting pearly/opaque shrimp observations.

King Arthur Baking, What is pate a choux? Why we love this versatile French dough:
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2022/10/24/what-is-pate-a-choux-why-we-love-this-versatile-french-dough
Steam expansion and the flour/egg structure that sets around the cavity.

Brennan's, Bananas Foster recipe:
https://www.brennansneworleans.com/bananas-foster
Used for the published ingredient sequence only. Its broad alcohol-removal
claim was not imported into training.

Betty Crocker, Pralines:
https://www.bettycrocker.com/recipes/pralines/203cd733-39db-4839-be7d-67597f403eee
First-party sugar/milk pecan-praline recipe, distinct from hard nut caramel.

Anson Mills, Corn:
https://ansonmills.com/grain_notes/13
Corn products, grits and hominy distinctions.

Validation
The four authored chapters pass tools/cuisine/validate.mjs. Targeted tests in
src/lib/prep-to-pass-editorial.test.ts guard the corrected principles across
read, recall and ordering surfaces. Derived data and production validation
belong to the release build, not this editorial task. This is a focused review
of the six newly illustrated topics, not certification of all legacy content
or a claim to know unpublished Brennan's production formulas.
