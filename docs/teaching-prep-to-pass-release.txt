THE COOK'S EYE: FROM PREPARATION TO SERVICE
23 October 2026

This continues the visual audit's six Next collection briefs (07 through 12).
It adds six studies to the existing Cook's Eye group, keeping one learning
destination in Brennan's > Kitchen studies > Illustrated kitchen studies.
The Plates library (/plates) and matching lesson, term and revealed card
surfaces use the same studies. Existing grade and content identities remain.

The six studies and their 27 numbered views
1. Blanch, cool, drain and finish: a prep-ahead green-bean example (4 views).
2. Coating and frying: breaded chicken versus a separate courgette batter
   method, with rack drainage and a measurement reminder (6 views).
3. Sugar: cooled soft-ball, firm-ball and hard-crack samples, separately
   compared with three caramel colours (6 views).
4. Choux: panade, egg-enriched paste, piping and a hollow baked shell (4 views).
5. Panisse, grits and polenta: chickpea versus maize and the distinction
   between the ingredient and a creamy, set or fried serving form (3 views).
6. Beurre blanc: reduction, butter incorporation, cohesive sauce and a
   separate failed emulsion comparison (4 views).

The Cook's Eye now contains twelve studies. The full companion collection
contains thirty studies and 119 views; the twenty reference plates remain

Accuracy and artwork
These original generated illustrations teach general culinary methods.
They do not prescribe Brennan's unpublished quantities, production formulas
or final plating. The pimento-cheese grit cake in the house notes is explicitly
distinguished from the creamy grits example. Choux is a general pastry study.
Ingredients, allergens, doneness and safety cannot be inferred from the image.
The keys explain which additional checks the learner must make.

All six images were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and inspected
individually by two reviewers. Choux paste was corrected to a hanging taper;
grits and polenta were corrected to creamy ground-maize textures. Existing
artwork was not replaced. Twelve versioned full/thumbnail WebPs total
1,057,222 bytes and remain outside the app's offline core precache.
Full images are 1536 by 1024; thumbnails 420 by 280. Food is shown in neutral
light with ivory, forest-green and brass framing and subtle compass corners.
