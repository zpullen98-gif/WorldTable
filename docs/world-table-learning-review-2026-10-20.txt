WORLD TABLE: EDITORIAL AND LEARNING-FLOW REVIEW
20 October 2026

Purpose
Make it easier to find a starting point, understand the material, practise it
in a manageable sitting, and apply it in the kitchen or at the table.
Retain the existing exploration, hospitality and subtle mysticism aesthetic.

Scope
The interface review visited 34 principal routes, including all four levels,
Brennan's menu, fall notes and final plates, Foundations, Cuisine, Practise,
quizzes, Library, recipes, Lexicon, techniques, pantry, course, service,
safety, palate, reference plates, Cook at home, Family, progress, costing,
preps, prep board, waste, guest menu and More.

The content review examined the structure and connections of 120 Foundations
terms, 179 Cuisine entries across 12 chapters, and 17 fall training items.
Detailed corrections concentrated on foundational cooking methods and
selected safety lessons, including their associated cards and questions.
This was not a line-by-line independent verification of all 1,844 recipes,
every historical claim, every external video or every live restaurant fact.

What changed
1. Home explains each level and offers a direct Today's study shortcut.
   A chosen level changes the study focus; it does not lock other material.
2. Practise has subject jumps and a searchable deck/round directory.
   Large general collections start with twenty cards by default, with an
   explicit whole-deck choice. Due queues and ordered teaching runs retain
   their existing rules. A large catalogue is no longer an implied sitting.
3. Revealed flashcards connect back to the relevant dish, term or lesson.
   Back returns to the same card and answer state.
4. Missing study data produces an honest partial-queue message and recovery
   controls. Quiz failures release their controls. A reload option handles
   failed module imports that a browser retains until a fresh document.
5. Techniques can be searched and grouped. Library chapters are searchable.
   Recipe browsing links directly to that chapter directory. House videos
   are folded behind a disclosure and keep their open state on return.
6. Lexicon searches, categories and learner position survive a lesson visit.
   A narrowly filtered quiz asks about the selected material instead of
   silently switching to the entire Lexicon.
7. Pantry selection and empty results have clearer next actions and larger
   touch targets. Reading and filtering do not change the saved pantry.
8. Recipes have shortcuts to ingredients, method, result checks, pairings,
   personal notes and videos. Backup guidance points to the real tools.
9. More contains a learning guide, linked from Home, Today and Library.
   Six applied journeys connect identify, compare, describe and practise:
   reading a plate; heat and tenderness; sauce families; pasta and place;
   guest questions; dessert texture. Each ends with an oral self-check.
10. Offline messages distinguish core installed reading from images and
    cooking lessons cached as used. Videos and external sources need the
    network. A missing page has correctly named recovery destinations.
11. Cook at home explains a failed index or lesson download and provides
    recovery rather than leaving an indefinite loading message.

Editorial corrections
The authored definitions and their original Floor Deck cards now distinguish
curing, smoking, cooking and ready-to-eat status; make frying coatings
optional; avoid constant-tossing rules for sauteing; distinguish prosciutto
crudo and cotto; and describe cheek tenderness without a universal duration.

Cuisine corrections address sauteing, smoking, confit storage, flambe safety
and a holding-control question. Cold smoke is not taught as a kill step;
seafood-specific smoking figures are labelled as such. A small contained
pan fire is distinguished from spreading fire. A low holding-temperature
reading does not create a new time allowance. Related recall questions and
cards were corrected with their lessons.

Primary references are retained with the relevant material. They include
the Culinary Institute of America, the Escoffier-hosted Gisslen methods
chapter, USDA FSIS, FDA seafood guidance and Food Code, US Fire
Administration, the Parma consortium, Levoni, and Meat & Livestock Australia.
The FDA model code is not presented as proof of local adoption.

Preservation
Stable recipe slugs, lesson/card/question identities, personal progress,
house ingredients and uploaded menu records remain intact. The original
archive and raw extraction are unchanged. No house recipe quantities or
actual plating were invented. The final-plate images keep their illustrative
caption. The same day/night tokens and five primary tabs are retained.

Verification
Focused tests cover short sessions, whole-deck choice, answer-only lesson
links, return-to-card state, searching and returning, narrow quiz pools,
failure recovery, personal notes, chapter discovery and disclosure state.
Responsive checks cover 320, 390 and 1120 pixel widths in day and night
service. Accessibility scans cover the changed reading and study surfaces.
The release also runs the source check, full unit suite, data derivation
checks, production build verification and the public site's release gates.
Execution evidence is retained in this chat's dated output folder.

Next editorial priorities
Review the archive in small cuisine or technique collections, beginning
with recipes the learner actually cooks, to validate quantities, yields and
doneness checks against authoritative recipe sources. Confirm restaurant
preparation, substitutions and plating with the current kitchen. These are
separate evidence tasks; a clearer interface does not establish those facts.
