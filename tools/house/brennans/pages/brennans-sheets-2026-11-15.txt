Source: the owner's Brennan's sheets, photographed by the owner on 15 November 2026 and transcribed the same day (edition 2026-11-15): seven dish sheets (the listed ingredients, the sheet's dietary notes and the chef's notes) and both pages of the Wines by the Glass sheet, as the sheets print them, spelling included; research/menu-2026-11-15-sheets.md holds the whole transcript and research/menu-2026-11-15-photos the photographs that were saved
Brennan's, read on 2026-11-15. Seven dishes and seventeen wines by the glass. The sheets print no price for a dish or a wine.
The sheets head each dish's last block with the chef's first name; it is headed "The chef's notes" here, since nothing in this house names a living person. A letter in square brackets was cut off at the edge of a photograph and is completed from context. The glass sheet prints a dash between the grape and the wine; it is written here as a colon.
Four dishes replace four: Steak Tartare with Rye replaces the Steak Tartare Cannoli; Tuna Crudo replaces the Louisiana BBQ Lobster; Fish Bouillabaisse replaces the Redfish Veronique; Chocolate Cremeux replaces The Snickers. Three are additions: Swordfish Grenobloise, Agnolotti del Plin, Lamb Shank en Croute.

== Steak Tartare with Rye (dish; replaces the Steak Tartare Cannoli) ==
Listed ingredients:
  Raw Beef Tenderloin
  Tartare Dressing (egg yolk, dijon, caper, shallot, hot sauce, parsley, black pepper)
  Montreal Seasoning (Black pepper, carraway, coriander, chili flake, mustard seed)
  Vital Farms Egg Yolk
  Rye Tuile
Sheet dietary notes: Egg, Gluten
The chef's notes:
  Montreal steak spice originated in the 1940s at Schwartz's Hebrew Delicatessan when a cook used his deli's traditional smoked-meat pickling spices on a steak for his lunch
  In the late 1800's, steak tartare was originally called beefsteak a L'Americaine before a version was created by Auguste Escoffier using tartar sauce, leading to the name a la tartare
  Vital Farms is a collection of small egg farms that share practices of ethical farming
  Rye is a grain which is closely related to wheat known for its earthy, nutty flavor

== TUNA CRUDO (Tasting menu, dish; replaces the Louisiana BBQ Lobster) ==
Listed ingredients:
  Tuna
  Carrot Ginger Aguachile
  Salsa Seca
  Burnt Orange Segments
  Coriander
  Pickled Pearl Onions
  Maldon Salt
Sheet dietary notes: Alumn, Coriander, Chilis, Sesame, Gluten
The chef's notes:
  Aquachile is a traditional Mexican from the coastal state of Sinaloa usually made by submerging fresh fish in a blend of lemon juice and spices.
  Aquachile translates to "Chili Water"
  Salsa Seca is a Mexican condiment made from dried roasted chilis, tortilla chips, seeds and nuts
  Coriander is the Cilantro seed
  Coriander aversion is the strong perception that the herb taste like soap, dirt, or bugs caused by the chemical compound called Aldehydes.

== Fish Bouillabaisse (Tasting menu, dish; replaces the Redfish Veronique) ==
Listed ingredients:
  Stuffed Fish Terrine
  Bouillabaisse sauce
  Chickpea Panisse
  Confit Fennel
  Shrimp Mousse
  Preserved Lemon Aioli
  Fennel Frond Garnish
Sheet dietary notes: Shellfish, Dairy, Eggs, Pepper, Fin Fish, Alum, Alcohol, Nightshades, Gluten Contact
The chef's notes:
  Bouillabaisse is a traditional French fish stew usually served with crusty bread and a rouille
  Rouille is a thick garlicy Provencal French sauce made from olive oil egg yolks and breadcrumbs, saffron. It means Rust in French

== Chocolate Cremeux (Tasting menu, dish; replaces The Snickers) ==
Listed ingredients:
  Chocolate Cremeux
  Cocoa Nib Crumble
  Lemon Olive Oil
  Maldon Salt
Sheet dietary notes: Dairy, Eggs, Chocolate, Gelatin
The chef's notes:
  Cremuex is a rich, smooth French dessert cream made by emulsifying a pouring custard with chocolate or other flavorings and setting with gelatin
  The word "Cremeux" literally translates to "creamy"
  Maldon salt is a famous high end salt harvested from the coastal town of Maldon Essex, England

== Swordfish Grenobloise (dish; an addition) ==
Listed ingredients:
  Swordfish Fillet
  Leek Fondue
  Brown Butter
  Lemon
  Fried Capers
  Leidenheimer Croutons
  Parsley
Sheet dietary notes: Fish, Dairy, Shellfish (trace), Gluten (trace)
The chef's notes:
  Fish Grenobloise is a preparation from Grenoble, France. Being a landlocked area, fres[h] fish was hard to acquire, so they would top it with a strong sauce of brown butter, lemo[n] segments, and capers to mask any off flavors
  Leek fondue is a classic French side dish made by slowly cooking thinly sliced leeks in butter until meltingly tender, then enriching them with heavy cream or crème fraîche
  Swordfish is known for its dense, meaty texture and mild, slightly sweet flavor

Listed ingredients:
  Lamb Shank Daube
  Puff Pastry
  Spiced Lamb Jus
Sheet dietary notes: Gluten, Egg
The chef's notes:

== Brennan's Wines by the Glass (two pages; seventeen wines, no price printed) ==

Sparkling
  Argyle Brut 2022 Willamette Valley, OR. Champagne method sparkling wine from Willamette Valley, Oregon. It is a blend of Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Meunier. Notes of brioche, baked apple, cherry, and baking spices
  Pierre Sparr Rosé Brut NV Cremant d'Alsace, FR. This is a wine made in the champagne method. It is pinot noir rosé with notes of raspberry & strawberry. It comes from Alsace, France, which is located next to Germany.
  Piper-Heidsieck "Essential" Brut NV Champagne, France. Notes of pear, citrus and orange blossom. The palate also reveals aromas of toasted bread, fresh almonds and mint. The assemblage is 44% Pinot Noir, 34% Meunier, & 22% Chardonnay. This is our house champagne.
  Rare Champagne 2012 Champagne, France (Coravin). Notes of pear, citrus, brioche, tropical fruit, and salinity. This wine is only produced as a vintage champagne and only during the best vintages. In 40 years, Champagne Rare has only declared 14 vintages.

Rosé
  Grenache Blend: Minuty "Prestige" 2023. Provence, France. Light & Crisp with notes of red berries, citrus, and a hint of salinity. This is a grenache dominant blend. Provence is in southeastern France bordered by the Mediterranean Sea to the south.

Whites
  Albariño: Pazo das Brujas Rías Baixas Spain 2025. Crisp wine with notes of lime, green apple, under ripe pineapple, and sea salt. Lite and lively. This wine come from Galicia in the Northwest, it has a cooler and wetter climate than the rest of Spain.
  Chardonnay: Gainey Estate Vineyards Sta. Rita Hills CA 2023. Notes of honey, apples, stone fruit, and oak. The Santa Rita Hills are in central California and receive a lot of cool breezes from the Pacific that temper the risk of grapes becoming over ripe.
  Riesling: C.H. Berres "Old Vines" 2022 Mosel, Germany. This is an off-dry (slightly sweet) wine with a floral nose and notes of kiwi, and apple. This is complimented with high acidity and fresh minerality.
  Sauvignon Blanc: Famille Durand 2025, Loire Valley, FR. This wine is crisp with notes of citrus, white flowers, and delicate minerality. Most of the fruit come from Sancerre.
  Pinot Gris: Lemelson Vineyards "Tikka's Run" Willamette Valley OR 2022. Notes of pear, peach, & lemon. With a velvety, soft, and full bodied texture.
  Chardonnay (Coravin): Louis Jadot "Les Pucelles" Puligny-Montrachet 2008. Notes of lemon cream pie and vanilla. This wine vibrant, rich and lush. Les Pucelles is premier cru burgundy vineyard. That is in very close proximity to the Grand Crus of Montrachet.

Reds
  Grenache Blend: Domaine de Durban 2024 Southern Rhone, France. Tasting of Black cherry & herbs de provence. If a guest likes Chateauneuf-du-Pape, they will enjoy this wine. It is a Grenache blend with Syrah and Mourvèdre.
  Barbera: La Miraja "Le Masche" Barbera d'Asti Superiore 2023 Piemonte IT. Notes of red cherry, plum, blackberry, licorice and a hint of balsamic. Barbera is a low tannin grape that is versatile and can pair with a lot of food
  Pinot Noir: Damien Martin 2023 Burgundy, France. Notes of strawberry, black cherry, and raspberry. Round and rich on the palate, with intense fruit and silky tannins. All the fruit is sourced from the Mâconnais in southern burgundy.
  Cabernet Sauvignon Blend: Moulin d'Issan 2021 Bourdeaux, France. Notes of blackberries, cassis, violets and spice. The blend is cabernet and merlot. This wine is a second label of the 3rd growth by Château d'Issan.
  Cabernet Sauvignon: Kokomo Sonoma County CA 2022. Notes of blackberries, cassis, and vanilla. Full bodied, velvety texture, and soft tannins. Most of the fruit is sourced from Alexander Valley in Sonoma County.
  Pinot Noir (Coravin): Domaine de Courcel "Les Grands Epenots" Pommard 2007. Notes of dark cherry, plum, and spice. This wine is almost 20 years old, so the tannins have softened and the fruit is very mellow and earthy.

Seventeen wines: four sparkling, one rosé, six whites, six reds; three by Coravin (Rare 2012, Jadot Les Pucelles 2008, de Courcel Les Grands Epenots 2007).
