Source: the owner's Brennan's sheets, photographed by the owner on 22 November 2026 and transcribed the same day (edition of November 2026, Thanksgiving): sheet 1, the Thanksgiving Day menu; sheet 2, "Dinner Pairing Wines". The photographs were not saved as files; the transcript is research/menu-2026-11-22-sheets.md.
Brennan's, read on 2026-11-22. One fixed price menu for Thanksgiving Day (Thursday 26 November 2026) and the five pours of the dinner tasting's wine pairing.
A letter in square brackets was cut off at the right edge of the photograph and is completed from context; a word may be lost at each cut and is to confirm at lineup. The sheet prints a hyphen between "$80" and "choice"; it is written as a colon here. No allergen mark is printed on the Thanksgiving sheet and no wine is printed on it. The pairing sheet prints no vintage and no price for any wine.

The photograph ends under the Cranberry Sauce; whether more sides follow is to confirm.

Nineteen lines: six appetizers, six entrées, three desserts and four sides for the table. Three of them are dishes the house menus already print: the turtle soup, the seafood gumbo and the Bananas Foster.

== Sheet 2: Dinner Pairing Wines (a plain typed sheet; five pours, no vintage and no price printed) ==
  1st Course: Charles Lafitte Champagne. Vibrant & fresh with crisp apple & honey notes.
  2nd course: Domaine Weinbach Sylvaner. Alsace FR. Ripe pear with green notes and a lush texture.
  3rd course: Domaine du Vieux Lazaret Blanc. Châteauneuf-du-Pape FR. Grenache Blanc Blend notes of white flowers, lemon, & apricot with a full-bodied texture.
  4th course: Paul Hobbs "Coombsville" Cabernet Sauvignon. Napa, CA. Notes of blackberry, cassis, sage, and tobacco. Elegant, silky tannins.
  5th course: Domaine la Tour Vieille. Banyuls, FR. Notes of sweet jammy dark fruits and exotic spice.

Five pours, numbered by course as printed. Two are new to the house's records: the Domaine Weinbach Sylvaner and the Domaine du Vieux Lazaret Blanc.
