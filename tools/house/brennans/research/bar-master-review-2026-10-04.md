# The bar, master review of 4 October 2026: facts the drink cards now use

Written for the master bartender's review of My Menu. Each fact names how it is known: a web search
run on 4 October 2026 (the result pages named), or expert knowledge (a head bartender's working
knowledge, high confidence, the pattern every bar book prints). Where a fact touches the house's own
build, the house build stays a lineup question; these are the bottles and the classics, never the
measures Brennan's uses.

## Elio Perrone Bigaro (the OBX)

- Bigaro is a softly sparkling, gently sweet pink wine from Elio Perrone in Piedmont, a blend of
  Brachetto and Moscato, about 5 percent alcohol, from the hills of Agliano d'Asti and Nizza Monferrato.
  Search, 4 October 2026: K&L Wine Merchants product page (SKU 1851680), Flatiron Wines product page,
  Rare Wine Co technical sheet for Elio Perrone. Whether the bottle on the bar is that vintage stays
  the sommelier's lineup question.

## Cathead (Five Minutes More)

- Cathead Distillery, Jackson, Mississippi, founded in 2010, the first legal distillery in Mississippi;
  its flavoured vodka is Cathead Satsuma Mandarin Vodka. Search, 4 October 2026: Visit Jackson
  directory, catheaddistillery.com Our Story and shop pages.

## Licor 43 (Five Minutes More)

- Licor 43 is made in Cartagena, Spain, from a secret family recipe of 43 ingredients, which gives
  it its name; citrus and vanilla lead. Search, 4 October 2026: licor43.com FAQs, Wikipedia (Licor 43).

## Green Chartreuse (Five Minutes More)

- Green Chartreuse is made by the Carthusian monks in the French Alps from a secret recipe of 130
  plants and bottled at 55 percent alcohol. Search, 4 October 2026: Maison du Whisky, Chartreuse
  origin article; cocktail-society.com Chartreuse explainer.

## Monkey 47 (the Flamingo)

- Monkey 47 is a Black Forest gin made with 47 botanicals and bottled at 47 percent. Search,
  4 October 2026: Pernod Ricard brand page for Monkey 47.

## Lillet Blanc (the Riviera, To Each His Own)

- Lillet Blanc comes from Podensac, in Bordeaux, created in 1872: Bordeaux white wine (Sauvignon Blanc
  and Semillon) blended with citrus liqueurs. Search, 4 October 2026: Wikipedia (Lillet).

## Rittenhouse (Rumors are Flying)

- Rittenhouse is a straight rye from Heaven Hill in Kentucky, bottled in bond at 100 proof, aged at
  least four years. Search, 4 October 2026: heavenhilldistillery.com Rittenhouse page. Who aged the
  Brennan's barrel, and for how long, stays the bar's lineup question.

## Chandon Garden Spritz

- Chandon sells the Garden Spritz ready made: its Brut sparkling wine from Mendoza, Argentina, blended
  with a bitter orange liqueur made from orange peel, herbs and spices; Chandon is the sparkling wine
  house Moet and Chandon founded outside Champagne. Search, 4 October 2026: Decanter review (Chandon,
  Garden Spritz, Mendoza, Argentina); chandon.com Garden Spritz page. Whether our bar pours the bottled
  product stays the bar's lineup question. The menu prints a build, Bitter Orange Liqueur and Chandon
  Brut Sparkling Wine, and the Chandon Brut sold in the United States is the California wine, so the
  card says only that the ready made bottle is from Mendoza and leaves which ours is to the bar.

## Zero proof (the Personality)

- Zero proof is a marketing name, not a regulated figure: in the United States a drink may be called
  non-alcoholic at under 0.5 percent alcohol, and dealcoholized wines are often labelled at up to 0.5.
  Expert knowledge.
- Wolffer Estate's non-alcoholic sparkling rose, Spring in a Bottle, made in Rheinhessen, is listed at
  0.5 percent alcohol. Search, 4 October 2026: "Wolffer Estate Zero Proof Sparkling Rose alcohol by
  volume" (wolffer.com Spring in a Bottle Rose Non-Alc page; thezeroproof.com; wine.com). The menu prints
  "Wolffer Estate Zero Proof Sparkling Rose" without naming the bottle, so whether ours is that wine, and
  the figure on its label, stay the bar's lineup question; until the bar has read the label, the
  Personality is not offered to a guest who can have no alcohol at all.

## Peychaud's on Royal Street (the Sazerac)

- Antoine Peychaud kept his apothecary at what was then 123 Royal Street, 437 Royal Street in today's
  numbering, the same block as Brennan's at 417. Search, 4 October 2026: the Sazerac Company's Our
  Story page (sazerac.com), neworleans.com Sazerac history; and dossier-the-bar.md section 5 (medium,
  tell it as a story). The house has not confirmed it wants servers to tell it: it stays a lineup check.
- The tale that Peychaud served drinks in an egg cup, a coquetier, and so gave us the word cocktail is
  folklore: the word was defined in print in 1806, in Hudson, New York. Dossier-the-bar.md section 5
  (high).
- Sazerac Rye, Peychaud's and Herbsaint are all Sazerac Company brands. Dossier-the-bar.md section 5.

## Herbsaint and absinthe

- Herbsaint takes its name from herbe sainte, the Creole name for wormwood; it was created in 1934 to
  stand in for absinthe, which the United States banned from 1912 until 2007. Dossier-the-bar.md
  sections 5 and 6 (high).
- A rinse: the glass is coated with the spirit and the excess poured out, so the drink takes its aroma.
  The Sazerac Company's own recipe coats the glass with Herbsaint and discards the excess.
  Dossier-the-bar.md section 5.

## Irish coffee, the story

- Irish coffee is credited to Joe Sheridan, chef at the Foynes flying boat terminal in County Limerick,
  in the winter of 1942 to 1943; the Buena Vista Cafe in San Francisco recreated it in 1952 and pours
  Tullamore D.E.W. Tullamore D.E.W. carries the initials of Daniel Edmund Williams, who rose to run and
  then own the distillery; he did not found it (it was established in 1829). Dossier-the-bar.md section 8.
- An Irish coffee is built in the glass: the sugar stirred into the hot coffee until it dissolves, then
  the whiskey, then the cream floated on top; it is not stirred once the cream is on. Expert knowledge
  (the Buena Vista and Foynes build, printed in every bar book); dossier-the-bar.md section 8.

## Milk punch, two kinds

- The New Orleans milk punch is the cold, shaken, unclarified kind; the older English milk punch curdles
  milk with spirit and citrus and strains it clear. Dossier-the-bar.md section 2 (high).

## Smaller words a server is asked

- Demerara is a light brown cane sugar with a toffee note. Expert knowledge.
- Allspice dram is a rum liqueur flavoured with allspice berries, the berry that tastes of clove,
  cinnamon and nutmeg together. Expert knowledge.
- Earl Grey is black tea scented with bergamot, a fragrant citrus. Expert knowledge.

## Classics guests ask for that the list does not print

- A Ramos Gin Fizz, a Vieux Carre and a French 75 are New Orleans classics the current list does not
  print; the Ramos takes a long, hard shake. Dossier-the-bar.md section 13. Whether the bar makes them
  on request, and at what price, is a manager's lineup question.
