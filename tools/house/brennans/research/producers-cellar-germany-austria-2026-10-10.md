## Method

No new web search ran for this pass. The session's shared WebSearch budget (200 calls a turn, shared by every agent) was already spent when this group began: the first two queries (Dönnhoff of Oberhausen, its founding and family; Weingut Hexamer of Meddersheim and its Rheingrafenberg) were each refused with "this turn's web search budget is used up". WebFetch was tried once, on the page that mattered most for the least documented estate (www.weingut-hexamer.de), and failed at the network egress with "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.weingut-hexamer.de". The limit was not worked around.

So this file is built from what the repositories already hold, and every item says which of four kinds it is:
- [search, earlier]: a live search made by an earlier pass and recorded in this repo with the pages it named. The passage is that pass's record of the page, not a fresh reading of it. Two files hold these for this group: research/components-wines-2026-10-05.md (search 48, Kracher: corneyandbarrow.com, kracher.at, vintageandvine.com) and research/bottle-facts-cards-a-2026-10-04.json and research/bottle-facts-cards-c-2026-10-04.json (the PIUS Auslese pages at wallywine.com and moorebrothers.com, and the Kracher 2017 product notes at decantalo.com, buzzwines.com and cellartracker.com, all searched on 4 October 2026 and mixed by that pass with its own knowledge).
- [house]: the house's own documents: the Binwise list as pasted on 3 October 2026 (tools/house/brennans/pages/binwise-wine-list-2026-10-03.txt), the shipped wine list static/shared/packs/brennans-new-orleans.winelist.v1.json (producer lines and tells), and the pack static/shared/packs/brennans-new-orleans.v1.oothouse.json (the ten wines of this group in full, their say lines and service notes). Where a pack field was written from an earlier pass's knowledge, the item says so.
- [Codex]: the Sommelier's Codex's own corpus: the producer records in sommelierscodex/js/data-producers.js and the question and primer records in data-questions.js, data-intro.js, data-advanced.js, data-master.js, data-primers.js, data-primers-advanced.js and reference.js. They were written to the Codex's refutation rule ("AN EMPTY FIELD IS A TRUE ANSWER"), but they are not independent web sources. Where a quoted Codex passage carried a dash or an arrow, it is written here as a colon or a comma.
- [expert]: standard wine knowledge, used only where certain and never for a date, a number, a person or a relation that is not also sourced above.
