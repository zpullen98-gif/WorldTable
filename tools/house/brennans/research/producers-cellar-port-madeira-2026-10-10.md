So every fact below comes from a source already in the repositories, quoted exactly with its path, and each item says which of four kinds it is:
- [search, earlier]: a live web search made by an earlier pass and recorded in this repo with the pages it named. The passage is that pass's record of the page, not a fresh reading. Two files hold these for this group: research/components-wines-2026-10-05.md (searches 31 Taylor Fladgate, 32 Sandeman, 33 Niepoort, 34 the Symingtons, Graham's and Dow's, 36 Madeira) and research/bottle-facts-cards-c-2026-10-04.json (the Feist card from vintageport.se, the Broadbent card from broadbent.com, winespectator.com and an ebay.com listing, the Henriques & Henriques card from henriquesehenriques.pt, alpenz.com and cellartracker.com, all searched on 4 October 2026).

- [Codex]: the Sommelier's Codex's own corpus: the producer records in sommelierscodex/js/data-producers.js and question records in data-intro.js, data-advanced.js and data-master.js.
