# The kitchen: Brennan's as a master chef reads it

## Key facts
- (pdf p50) Brennan's opened in 1946 and has been at 417 Royal Street since 1956; 2026 is the 80th year, with $80 breakfast and dinner tasting menus through the year (menus read 26 September 2026).
- (pdf p49) Hours: breakfast and lunch Mon to Fri 9 to 2, Sat and Sun 8 to 2; dinner nightly 6 to 10. Dinner sides are $12 each; turtle soup, gumbo and the Creole Caesar appear at both meals.
- (pdf p49; search, Axios New Orleans 4 June 2025) Kris Padalino has been executive chef since June 2025, the house's first female executive chef in eight decades, after eight years as pastry chef and executive sous chef; she wants tableside cooking back.
- (expert, correcting the draft) Michael Roussel started in 1956, rose from dishwasher to executive chef and died in 2005 still in post after 49 years; Lazone Randolph (dishwasher from 1965) led the kitchen until the 2013 closure. The house promotes from within.
- (pdf p33, p49; search, house Traditions page and Ralph Brennan via Axios) Bananas Foster was created at Brennan's in 1951 and named for Richard Foster; the house's own telling credits Ella Brennan with chef Paul Blangé, which the guide omits. 2026 is its 75th year. $14, minimum two.
- (pdf p34) The printed menu says rum; Brennan's published recipe also uses banana liqueur; follow the house procedure. Flaming removes only part of the alcohol (expert, USDA table: about 75 per cent retained), so the p47 line 'most of it burns off' is too generous.
- (pdf p49 versus search) The Eggs Hussarde pour on the $80 breakfast tasting is Charles Lafitte Brut in the guide but Brennan's Essential by Piper-Heidsieck on the house page and OpenTable; confirm before naming it.
- (pdf p6 to p7 versus search) The dinner tasting duck is cane syrup peaches and candied hazelnuts in the guide but a hazelnut-crusted dry-aged Rohan with sweet potato and winter squash on the indexed page; the lobster pour is Fichet Mâcon-Igé 2024 in the guide, Domaine Matrot Meursault 2023 on the indexed page. Read the printed card.
- (expert) Marchand de vin at Brennan's is a roux-based brown sauce with ham, mushroom, onion, garlic, stock and red wine, not a bare reduction; the guide's p2 wording 'a red wine and mushroom sauce' is the right one.
- (pdf p22; expert) In New Orleans 'bordelaise' usually means garlic butter, not the red wine and marrow sauce; confirm which the pecan bordelaise is before pairing wine.
- (expert, correcting the draft) Antoine's original Eggs Sardou has no spinach; the creamed spinach version is Brennan's own, and the 2026 plate (crispy artichokes, Parmesan spinach, Choron) is a variation on the house classic.
- (expert) Rohan is a proprietary D'Artagnan hybrid raised in New York, not a breed; cane syrup is lighter and sweeter than molasses; tasso is spiced smoked pork shoulder; en papillote literally means in paper and the banana leaf is the house substitution.
- (expert, correcting the draft) Débris is Louisiana French for scraps that Mother's Restaurant (1938) made famous, not coined; sauce Foyot is béarnaise with meat glaze named for the Restaurant Foyot in Paris, with no Gouffé or 1863 attribution; Luxardo re-founded at Torreglia in 1947, not 1946.
- (expert, replacing a disputed draft) Brennan's prints '100 percent turtle meat' because many turtle soups are stretched with veal, beef or pork; the Commander's Palace recipe cooks turtle in veal stock, not equal parts veal. Safe line: 'Ours is all turtle, nothing padding it out.'
- (expert, disputed draft corrected) The Fresno chilli (1952, Fresno, California) sits in the same 2,500 to 10,000 Scoville band as a jalapeño and a red one can be hotter, not milder; preserved and diluted in the mignonette it reads as gentle warmth.
- (pdf p10 to p11) The oyster BBQ sauce line describes the Manale style in general, not the house recipe; the contents of Brennan's 2026 sauce are unconfirmed. Safe line: 'a buttery, peppery New Orleans BBQ sauce'; Worcestershire and anchovy questions go to the kitchen.
- (pdf p13; expert) Egg yolk bottarga appears only on the Steak Tartare Cannoli on the current menus; no Ricotta Gnudi is listed, so do not call cured yolk a standing house preparation.
- (search, Narragansett etymology; Wikipedia) Succotash is Native American in origin, Southern by adoption; the guide's 'classic Southern side' should soften. Nueske's (Wittenberg, Wisconsin, 1933) smokes over applewood for at least 24 hours.
- (search, several Louisiana outlets) Maggie's Mushrooms is very likely Mushroom Maggie's Farm, St Francisville (Maggie Long and Cyrus Lester), but the link to Brennan's is inferred and must be confirmed by the chef; Tien Dat tofu could not be found by search, so say only 'made here in New Orleans'.
- (pdf p50 to p51; expert) Pronunciation changes: beurre blanc is burr BLAHN with no K; Brabant is bra-BAHN or the local bruh-BANT; refine Choron to sho-ROHN and marchand de vin to mar-SHAHN; the rest of the guide's table is sound.
