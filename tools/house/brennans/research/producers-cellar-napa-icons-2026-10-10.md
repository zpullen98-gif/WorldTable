Group napa-icons. Ten producers: Inglenook, the Rubicon we pour by the glass and sell by the bottle; Paul Hobbs, whose Coombsville Cabernet is the dinner tasting's Cabernet (the Jadot Beaune is its other red; see Verification 2026-10-10); and the eight famous Napa names of the California Cabernet and Napa pages: Opus One, Harlan Estate, Screaming Eagle, Dominus Estate, Heitz Cellar, Shafer Vineyards, Caymus Vineyards and Chateau Montelena, whose Chardonnay is on the white pages.
