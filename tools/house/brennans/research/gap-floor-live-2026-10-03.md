## 1. Who performs the tableside work

(search: southernfoodways.org, Gravy episode "Flambeed! The Art and Theater of Bananas Foster") The summary printed with the results names Chalaine Celestain as a Captain at Brennan's, "where she's worked for about nine years", and says "the role of captains is central to the tableside dining experience at the restaurant". The same result set said "Suited servers still fire it up tableside" and "Staff members spend months training to cook Bananas Foster tableside for guests". The second sentence reads like house or Gravy copy; which page it came from (brennansneworleans.com/traditions or the Gravy page) is not separable in the snippet.

What this settles: a captain tier exists at Brennan's, captains do tableside work, and the flambe is not something a new server does in the first weeks. The pdf (p49) says "ask at training who performs it (server or captain)"; the live answer is that the tableside flame belongs to trained staff after months of training, with the captain as the named tableside role. (expert, medium) Read together, a server should assume the captain, or a server who has passed the house's training, flames, and should not expect to light a pan until signed off. That inference is not a published rule.

Still unknown: the written house flambe procedure, the sign-off, the fuel, and whether any non-captain flames today.

(search: axios.com, 6 April 2026, "Brennan's throws a Bananas Foster bash") The courtyard party "includes a flame competition and the crowning of the Banana King". (expert) A flame competition among staff is consistent with more than one trained performer on the floor; it does not say who.

## 10. The Ralph Brennan Restaurant Group

(search: neworleans-food.com and brennansneworleans.com/ralph-brennan/) The group's restaurants: Brennan's, Napoleon House and Red Fish Grill in the French Quarter; Ralph's on the Park in Mid-City; Cafe NOMA inside the New Orleans Museum of Art; Jazz Kitchen Coastal Grill and Patio and Beignets Expressed in the Downtown Disney District at the Disneyland Resort. Red Fish Grill, on Bourbon Street, "has served well over three million" BBQ oysters. Ralph's on the Park is an 1860s cream clapboard building. Napoleon House dates to the mid-1800s and is known for the Pimm's Cup and the muffuletta. Cafe NOMA has been named among the best museum restaurants in America.

(expert, high) Mr. B's Bistro, which appeared in the link list, is Cindy Brennan's house, not Ralph's; Commander's Palace is the Garden District cousins' house. Neither is in the group.
