# Brennan's bar accuracy review, 15 October 2026

This is an evidence and training review of the saved house collection, not a new menu or a current availability claim. The collection contains 32 drink records: 26 with conventional alcoholic ingredients and 6 classified zero proof, including coffees and juice. Nine fall cocktails overlap those existing records. They are not nine additional house drinks. Printed names, IDs, ingredient specifications, prices, sections, meals, glasses and garnishes are preserved by these corrections.

## Evidence boundaries

The current saved menu and supplied fall sheet establish named ingredients. They generally do not establish measures, exact bottles, preparation sequence, finished drink alcohol concentration, garnish or glass. A classic recipe, manufacturer serving suggestion and published restaurant recipe are each useful teaching references; none automatically supplies the current house build. Positive flavor descriptions are suggestions from ingredients until the current preparation is tasted. A short menu list cannot prove ingredient absence.

## Verified corrections

### Chandon: distinguish product, country and method

The [Chandon U.S. Garden Spritz technical sheet](https://www.chandon.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-DomaineChandon_US-Library/default/dw916ccca0/pdf/CHANDON%20-%20Garden%20Spritz%20Tasting%20Notes.pdf) identifies an Argentine product. It describes second fermentation and ageing in tank, followed by blending with a bitter component before bottling. That contradicts the old comparison that made the house menu name prove California and the traditional method. The [producer introduction](https://www.chandon.com/en-us/gardenspritz-blog.html) also presents a prepared product.

Keep Brennan's printed Chandon Brut and bitter orange wording. Ask whether the bar builds the drink or pours a prepared product, then read its current label. The technical sheet is not proof of today's house SKU. Chandon has products in multiple markets; avoid a blanket country or method for the whole brand.

### Cap Corse and Lillet: name the category correctly

The official U.S. importer describes [Cap Corse Mattei Blanc](https://alpenz.com/product-cap_corse_blanc.html) as a quinquina with a mistelle base and cinchona. Its existing component already makes the important distinction from vermouth. Retain the printed Spoonbill specification, including the menu's word vermouth, as documentary text; teaching should use quinquina. Its presence also does not establish a dry finished cocktail.

[Lillet's product collection](https://www.lillet.com/fr-be/gammes/) identifies a wine based aperitif. Riviera's comparison previously called Lillet a liqueur; the corrected comparison distinguishes Lillet from Kir Royale's crème de cassis. Both the wine aperitif and sparkling wine contain alcohol. No finished strength ranking follows without measures.

### Kahlúa: caffeine is present, its total is unknown

The [Kahlúa producer FAQ](https://www.kahlua.com/en/faq/) confirms caffeine in the coffee liqueur and describes market dependent alcohol strengths. The printed Espresso Martini includes Kahlua, so its caffeine presence is not wholly unknown merely because the menu says chicory rather than espresso. Its total caffeine still depends on quantities and any additional coffee. Correct the ingredient lesson, producer note and retained guest answers together. Do not promise an effect on sleep or a caffeine free preparation.

### Published Milk Punch recipes exist

[Explore Louisiana's Brandy Milk Punch recipe](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/culinary/recipes/brandy-milk-punch-caribbean-milk-punch-cocktail) explicitly credits Brennan's Restaurant for the recipe and images. It is supplied material presented by the official state tourism authority, not an invented reconstruction. It includes measures and preparation. [Imbibe's 2015 recipe](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/brandy-milk-punch-recipe/) also credits Brennan's. Thus the saved answer saying no house recipe is published is too broad.

The correction acknowledges published versions while retaining the current saved menu as the house ingredient evidence. Do not import an older measured recipe into today's spec. Similarly, an eggnog comparison does not establish that the full house preparation is free of egg.

### Sazerac history: attribution and chronology

The [Sazerac Company timeline](https://www.sazerac.com/our-company/our-story/) separates Handy's 1869 purchase from its account of a later shift toward rye in 1885. This corrects the previous prose that collapsed the takeover and phylloxera narrative into the same moment. The source is a producer's historical account, not a contemporary first recipe. The [Sazerac House pamphlet](https://www.sazerachouse.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Sazerac_Pamphlet_SazHouse_RGB-Pages.pdf) gives the broader cognac shortage and rye account.

Teach Origin Story as Brennan's Cognac interpretation referring to that tradition, not a verified reconstruction of the first Sazerac. The current named products and the missing measures alone prevent an exact original recipe claim. Thompson's Dream's name is also not established by the menu; guessing Thompson Willett is not an appropriate factual guest answer.

### Method lessons and house confirmation

[Diageo Bar Academy on shaking](https://www.diageobaracademy.com/en-us/home/customer-service/mastering-the-art-of-cocktail-shaking) explains the common combining, chilling, dilution and aeration purposes. Its [stirring lesson](https://www.diageobaracademy.com/en-zz/home/bartender-skills-and-techniques/the-subtle-art-of-cocktail-stirring) supports a distinction based on control and desired texture. The old cloudy/clear slogan was too absolute, especially because many stirred drinks contain liqueurs. The revised cards teach method as recipe dependent, not derivable from a single ingredient. Stir time is not a universal endpoint.

The [Bar Academy carbonation discussion](https://www.diageobaracademy.com/en-us/home/cocktail-trends-and-festive-serves/who-needs-champagne) distinguishes mixing noncarbonated ingredients from sparkling additions. Use the specified recipe or prepared product instructions, and keep carbonated ingredients out of a sealed shaker. Avoid universal last-ingredient rules across every built drink. The source menu does not establish the house's final glass or sequence.

Clarification and barrel ageing notes are deliberately narrowed to evidence. The menu's clarified heavy cream neither supplies the exact house process nor establishes milk absence or a safe holding time. Likewise Brennan's Barrel Aged Rittenhouse does not identify the vessel, duration, operator or exact material aged. No storage extension or house ageing program is asserted by the corrections. Existing service material already identifies cream as milk; obtain the full recipe and actual handling for a dietary request.

### Cold pressing does not establish later processing

[FDA juice safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-juice-safety) distinguishes untreated juice from heat treated and otherwise treated juice. A cold extraction description cannot identify subsequent treatment or shelf life. Revive's strawberry, apple and pineapple remain the printed ingredients; supplier treatment and storage directions need confirmation.

## Legacy study contradictions removed

- Ralph's Coffee was labeled the gentlest coffee cocktail despite missing quantities; related Irish Coffee and Espresso Martini upsells repeated the ranking. The correction compares nut liqueurs with whiskey instead.
- Riviera, Chandon Garden Spritz and For Sentimental Reasons carried unsupported lower strength rankings. Sparkling format and flavor do not provide those measurements.
- Old Buttermilk Sky promised both preference and a completely alcohol absent alternative. The correction asks the guest's preference and points to checking the nonalcoholic bottle label.
- Bloody Mary and Bloody Bull comparisons inferred house mix contents or exclusions. The corrected language names printed ingredients and requests the full mix before a dietary assurance.
- Prisoner of Love's exact curdling method and shelf life were inferred. The correction separates a generic technique from the unconfirmed house process and explicitly retains milk.
- Classic comparisons inferred a flute, shaking method, or ingredient absence from genre. These now distinguish canon and house evidence.
