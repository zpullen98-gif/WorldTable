- chef-dishes-a-2 Grand Isle Jewel Oysters, p13: Grand Isle Jewels is a brand launched in April 2025 by JEDCO with Grand Isle off-bottom farmers; the oysters are raised in floating cages, tumbled and sorted, which the PDF omits.
- chef-dishes-a-5 Turtle sourcing: Brennan's uses farm-raised freshwater turtle from Louisiana Foods; Rushing stated in 2014 that no endangered species are used, only Louisiana farm-raised turtle.
- chef-dishes-a-6 Dinner tasting duck course, p6 to p7: The dinner tasting page as indexed by search lists course four as 'Hazelnut-Crusted Duck: 14-day dry-aged Rohan, brown butter and sweet potato purée, winter squash, sage, duck jus', not the cane-syrup peach version the PDF read on 26 September 2026.
- chef-dishes-a-7 Dinner tasting lobster pairing, p6: The dinner tasting page as indexed pairs the BBQ lobster with Domaine Matrot Meursault 2023, where the PDF names Fichet Mâcon-Igé 2024.
- chef-dishes-a-8 Petite filet pairing, p5: The live breakfast tasting page agrees with the PDF (Châteaumar 'Cuvée Vincent' Côtes du Rhône 2023, 4 oz) while an older Brennan's events page lists Stéphane Ogier 'La Rosine' Syrah 2016; the restaurant's own pages disagree.
- chef-dishes-a-9 Baked Apple, p4: Brennan's recipe page: apples are peeled, trimmed flat, cored with a melon baller and topped with about two tablespoons of a flour, butter, light brown sugar, cinnamon, rolled oat, raisin and pecan mix.
- chef-dishes-a-10 Seafood Gumbo, p15: Brennan's published Seafood Filé Gumbo uses a mahogany oil-and-flour roux, andouille, trinity with red and green pepper, garlic, okra, tomato, crab stock, and adds crab, shrimp and oysters in the last three minutes.
- chef-dishes-a-15 Crab claws, p12: Two published reviews (NoMenu, Tripadvisor) found the warm claws tough and the meat hard to pull from the cartilage, and said the menu did not signal they are served hot.
- chef-dishes-a-23 Kris Padalino: Padalino became executive chef in June 2025, trained at Le Cordon Bleu Pasadena, spent eight years at Brennan's as executive sous chef and pastry chef, has a maths and chemistry background and Sicilian roots, and wants tableside cooking back.
- chef-dishes-b-6 Eggs Owen: A third-party listing (Yelp item page) states Eggs Owen is named for founder Owen Brennan, who opened the restaurant in July 1946 and died in 1955; the restaurant's own menu does not say so.
- chef-dishes-b-7 Eggs Owen: An older published description of Eggs Owen lists two poached eggs and crispy fingerling potatoes, while the PDF and the live 2026 menu list a soft-boiled egg and a Creole-spiced potato.
- chef-dishes-b-13 Pecan Gulf Fish: Brennan's has served a Gulf Fish Amandine (meunière with almonds); the pecan version is the Louisiana nut substituted in the same lineage.
- chef-dishes-c-4 Bananas Foster Day 2026: Brennan's hosted a Bananas Foster Day party in its courtyard on 6 April 2026 with free tastings for the first 75 guests, a flame competition and the crowning of a Banana King; Richard Foster's grandsons attended.
- chef-dishes-c-5 Brennan's published recipe: Brennan's published Bananas Foster recipe (brennansneworleans.com/recipes/bananas-foster) uses banana liqueur and aged rum; the snippet lists 1 oz butter, half cup light brown sugar, quarter tsp cinnamon, 1.5 oz banana liqueur, 1.5 oz aged rum and half a banana per person.
- bar-signature-20 Bloody Bull origin: The Bull Shot (vodka and cold beef bouillon) is credited to the Caucus Club in Detroit in 1952, the Bloody Bull marries it with the Bloody Mary, and Brennan's is widely associated with and often credited as originator of the Bloody Bull as a breakfast drink, though the PDF only calls it 'a Brennan's breakfast classic'.
- somm-whites-1 Brennan's Essential by Piper-Heidsieck Extra Brut NV: The house Champagne is most likely Piper-Heidsieck's 'Essentiel' Extra Brut cuvée (launched around 2016, roughly 5 to 6 g/L dosage, about four years on lees, Pinot Noir-led blend with Meunier and Chardonnay, disgorgement date on the label) sold under a Brennan's co-branded label rather than a bespoke blend.
- somm-program-2 Grand Award year: The reopened Brennan's regained the Grand Award in the late 2010s, most likely around 2018, under wine director Braithe Tidwell, and the original Brennan's had held the award before its 2013 closure.
