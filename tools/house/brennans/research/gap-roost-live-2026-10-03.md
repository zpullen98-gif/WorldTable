The PDF (p39) places those two under the Sazerac service note and the cocktails page shows "premium variations at $20.00 and $40.00" under the Sazerac, so the two luxury Sazeracs belong to the Sazerac section of the cocktails page, not to the Luxury Roost Bar page, on present evidence.

(expert, medium) The three names are birds: flamingo, birdcage, spoonbill. Gap-fill-2 recorded bird-themed cocktails rolling out in June 2025, so this page is most likely the 2025 bird list kept on as the Roost's standing luxury set while the main list changed to the 1946 theme. Treat that as a reading, not a house statement.

(expert, high on the products) Chopin is a Polish potato vodka. Monkey 47 is a Black Forest dry gin at 47 percent. Rabbit Hole Dareringer is a Kentucky straight bourbon finished in PX sherry casks, from Louisville. Cap Corse Mattei Blanc is a Corsican quinquina aperitif wine. Hendrick's is a Scottish gin with cucumber and rose. These are product facts for the server's own understanding; the house's own descriptions are the three builds above.
