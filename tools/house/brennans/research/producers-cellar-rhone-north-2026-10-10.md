Expert knowledge: Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise is a vin doux naturel, a sweet wine whose fermentation is stopped by adding grape spirit so that it keeps the grape's sugar; it is not a late-harvest wine, and its grape is Muscat à Petits Grains. The dry red Beaumes-de-Venise is one of the named crus of the Southern Rhône, an appellation separate from the sweet Muscat. The village name is usually explained by the caves (baumes) in its rock and by the Comtat Venaissin, the old papal territory, not by Venice. Durban is said dur-BAHN, with the French u, as the pack prints it.
