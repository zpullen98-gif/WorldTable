# Producers in the cellar: the Central Coast, Oregon and the wider world (10 October 2026)

Group central-coast-oregon-world. Eleven producers: three Santa Barbara County Chardonnay houses (Gainey Estate Vineyards, whose Chardonnay we pour by the glass, Sandhi and Au Bon Climat); Piedrasassi, the Santa Barbara Syrah label; two Willamette Valley Pinot Noir houses (Ken Wright Cellars and Bergstrom); and five from the wider world: Kumeu River of Auckland, Penfolds of South Australia, Château Musar of Lebanon, Gaia Wines of Santorini and Inniskillin of the Niagara Peninsula.
